How Pouncity works

01.

Create your pet's diary

Sign in with your Google account, no password. Add the basics: name, breed, age, weight, a photo. Quirks and vet contact whenever you're ready.

02.

Get a diet & grooming plan

Tap once for a feeding plan and a grooming rhythm, tuned to your pet's breed, age & weight. General guidance, not vet advice — shown every time.

03.

One tap a day

Tap to say they were fed — logged with who and when, with a nudge if the day slips by. That's the whole habit, no chore.

04.

Hand it to any sitter

Share one link and they open the full diary — feeding, quirks, vet — read-only, with no account to make.
